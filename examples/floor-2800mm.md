# Example 2 — Floor to floor 2,800 mm, treads 300 mm

The tool's first answer: ceil(2,800 / 200) = 14 risers of 200.0 mm, 13 treads, total run 3,900 mm,
2R + T = 700 mm — legal but steep-feeling.
The kinder alternative: 15 risers of 186.7 mm, 14 treads x 300 mm = 4,200 mm run, 2R + T = 673 mm,
if the extra 300 mm of run fits the plan.

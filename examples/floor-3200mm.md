# Example 1 — Floor to floor 3,200 mm, treads 280 mm

Risers: ceil(3,200 / 200) = 16 -> actual riser 200.0 mm (at the maximum, acceptable).
Treads: 15 x 280 mm -> total run 4,200 mm (4.20 m) plus landings.
2R + T = 680 mm — slightly steep feel; 17 risers of 188.2 mm with 280 mm treads gives 656 mm and a
kinder stair if the run fits.
